# Key Takeaways: CISIAM Enforcement Case

**Wind Rose Holding Limited — Compliance Advisory**
Date: 6 August 2026 | Case: SFC Statement of Disciplinary Action — CISIAM (fined HK$6.8 million, s.194 SFO)
Classification: Compliance / Risk Management

## Summary

CISIAM, sole fund manager for Tahoe Life's private fund, committed 92% of assets to structured notes linked to bonds of its affiliate, Tahoe Group — breaching mandate limits. After Tahoe Group's 2020 default, NAV fell 57%. SFC fined CISIAM HK$6.8m for inadequate due diligence and risk oversight.

## 1. Root Cause Analysis (key takeaways)

- **No dubious-arrangement screening at deal inception** — failed to identify/assess red flags in an unnecessarily complex, investor-directed structure (breach: Code of Conduct GP2; SFC Circular 21 Nov 2019).
- **No segregation between investor instruction and independent judgement** — investments executed on investor instruction without independent due diligence; research reports were form-filling, not substantive (breach: GP2; FMCC 1.2(d), 3.11.1).
- **No automated pre-trade mandate checks** — breached 30% single-issuer concentration limit and investment-grade-only objective; no look-through to underlying reference assets for structured notes (breach: FMCC 3.1; Internal Control Guidelines s.VIII).
- **No post-trade risk monitoring** — no stress testing, no connected-party register, no senior management escalation/sign-off trail (breach: FMCC 1.2(d), 3.11.1).

## 2. Applicability

Relevant wherever a client/connected group can direct or heavily influence investment selection (single-investor/family-office mandates, insurer-linked mandates, engaged-principal discretionary accounts). Four risk vectors: **client onboarding** (economic-interest capture), **risk profiling & suitability** (machine-enforceable restrictions, look-through), **trade monitoring** (wrapper vs. underlying rating), **client information management** (connected-party cross-referencing).

## 3. Recommendations (headline actions)

1. **Onboarding/mandate:** capture connected-party relationships; document commercial rationale for client-directed investments; encode restrictions as structured data, not free text.
2. **Dubious arrangement screening:** mandatory checklist aligned to SFC's Nov 2019 red flags; block execution pending EDD + senior management written approval; maintain an auditable dubious-arrangement log.
3. **Independent judgement controls:** prohibit pass-through execution of client instructions; escalate client-directed trades to compliance/investment committee; segregate recommend/review duties.
4. **Restriction & concentration monitoring:** automated pre-trade hard blocks on mandate breach; look-through analysis for structured/linked notes; continuous post-trade monitoring with monthly Risk Committee attestation.
5. **Risk management framework:** formal fund-level risk policy (FMCC 1.2(d), 3.11.1); periodic stress testing of concentrated/connected exposures; connected-party register integrated with client information system.
6. **Governance:** senior management (not deal team) approves flagged/breaching arrangements in writing; independent compliance reviews at least quarterly; full documentation retained per record-keeping period.
7. **Training:** mandatory case-based training on red-flag recognition and limits of client instruction; reinforce that revenue/relationship considerations never override escalation or restriction enforcement.
