# HK Regulatory Compliance Digest — July 2026

**Wind Rose Holding Limited** · Covering 1–31 July 2026
Contact: billy.leung@windroseholdco.com | (852) 9629 9165

Key theme: cybersecurity and market-integrity enforcement dominated; SFC phased out OTPs in favour of phishing-resistant authentication.

## SFC

- **3 Jul — Ex-Yuanta Securities rep suspended 9 months.** Wong Tim Hi allowed two third parties to operate client accounts without written authorisation (Ching Lee Holdings market manipulation matter). *Takeaway: verify and document third-party trading authorisations in account-opening/KYC.* [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/enforcement-news/?searchByName=1&targetEntity=Wong+Tim+Hi) — `Client Onboarding & KYC` `Trade Monitoring & Surveillance` `Enforcement`

- **9 Jul — Circular 26EC35: OTPs discontinued for login/device binding.** Internet brokers and VA trading platforms must migrate to phishing-resistant authentication (passkeys, crypto-bound devices); large brokers move immediately, hard deadline 8 Jul 2027 for the rest. Senior management personally accountable for losses from cyber control failures. [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/doc?refNo=26EC35) — `Client Onboarding & KYC` `Client Information Management` `Trade Monitoring & Surveillance`

- **23 Jul — Mutual Recognition of Funds extended to Malaysia.** MOU with Securities Commission Malaysia; Malaysian REITs can cross-list in HK; wider product set (futures-based, leveraged/inverse, commodity ETFs) effective immediately; simplified dual-IPO framework follows Sep 2026. *Takeaway: review eligibility/disclosure for Malaysia-domiciled or dual-listed products.* [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR111) — `Licensing & Supervision`

- **24 Jul — Leveraged & inverse (L&I) product framework enhanced.** Adds single-stock L&I products on liquid HK mega-caps; new operational/disclosure requirements. *Takeaway: reassess suitability and risk-disclosure procedures.* [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR115) — `Risk Profiling & Suitability` `Licensing & Supervision`

- **24 Jul — Victory Securities fined $1.7m; RO suspended 3 months.** Inadequate scrutiny of stock ownership + failure to report suspected fraud to SFC, surfaced during a ramp-and-dump investigation. [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/enforcement-news/?searchByName=1&targetEntity=Victory+Securities+Company+Limited) — `Trade Monitoring & Surveillance` `Licensing & Supervision` `Enforcement`

- **27 Jul — China Industrial Securities Int'l Asset Mgmt fined $6.8m.** Failed to identify/address red flags in dubious arrangements; other private fund management failures. *Takeaway: revisit red-flag escalation and investment due-diligence records.* [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR116) — `Risk Profiling & Suitability` `AML/CTF` `Enforcement`

- **28 Jul — Luk Fook Securities fined $2.1m (cybersecurity).** Inadequate controls contributed to a Sep 2022 ransomware attack that disrupted trading/email/accounting for ~3 weeks. [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/enforcement-news/?searchByName=1&targetEntity=Luk+Fook+Securities+(HK)+Limited) — `Client Information Management` `Trade Monitoring & Surveillance` `Enforcement`

- **30 Jul — HK$125m frozen at Futu Securities International.** Restriction notice over suspected false/artificial demand in an IPO; Futu itself not under investigation. [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/enforcement-news/?searchByName=1&targetEntity=Futu+Securities+International+(Hong+Kong)+Limited) — `Trade Monitoring & Surveillance` `Enforcement`

- **31 Jul — Sir Dickson Poon found culpable of insider dealing (MMT).** Insider dealing in Dickson Concepts shares ahead of disclosure of a ~HK$1.15bn PayPal/Honey Science windfall; Tribunal also examined a 7-week disclosure delay. [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR121) — `Trade Monitoring & Surveillance` `Enforcement`

**Enforcement watch:** Confirms the above as the substantive new July items; no new licence revocations or fresh worldwide freezing/injunction orders identified. Court of Appeal (21 Jul) dismissed a further appeal on the HK$1.8bn asset preservation order against Ms Zong Fuli / Jian Hao Ventures Ltd (earlier proceedings) — being checked against prior month's coverage.

## HKMA

- **15 Jul — Bank scam alert.** Public warning on fraudulent websites, phishing emails, spoofed internet-banking pages impersonating licensed banks. Reiterates banks never request login/OTP by phone, email or SMS. [Source](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/07/20260715-3/) — `Client Information Management` `AML/CTF`

- **24 Jul — Complaints Watch Issue No. 28.** Trends in customer complaints against authorized institutions; recurring service/control gaps highlighted. *Takeaway: benchmark complaint-handling data and escalation triggers against the themes raised.* [Source](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/07/20260724-3/) — `Client Information Management` `Licensing & Supervision`
