# HK Regulatory Compliance Digest — June 2026

**Wind Rose Holding Limited** · Covering 1–30 June 2026
Contact: billy.leung@windroseholdco.com | (852) 9629 9165

Key theme: SFC and HKMA both issued parallel circulars on AI-enabled cyber risk within a day of each other.

## SFC

- **1 Jun — XHK Limited fined HK$2.5m.** Accounting errors caused liquid capital deficits of HK$3.6–32.3m over four months; client/non-client money mishandled. *Takeaway: reinforces focus on financial resources reporting and client asset controls.* [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR82) — `Trade Monitoring` `Client Money Controls` `Licensing & Supervision`

- **2 Jun — Circular 26EC32: AI-enabled cyberattack preparedness.** Cites a 27% YoY rise in cyber incidents; firms must assess readiness for AI-enabled attacks. *Takeaway: review cyber vulnerability assessments now.* [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/intermediaries/supervision/doc?refNo=26EC32) — `Client Information Management` `Licensing & Supervision`

- **9 Jun — Insider dealing sentencing.** Wong Pak Ming sentenced to 5 months' imprisonment (Pegasus Entertainment Holdings shares); ~HK$474,000 fine + investigation costs. [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR92) — `Enforcement` `Trade Monitoring & Surveillance`

- **11 Jun — Worldwide freezing injunction ~HK$146.9m.** Against Lo Kai Bong and Major Success Group Ltd, part of SFC s.214 proceedings; next hearing 26 Aug 2026. [Source](https://www.prudent.hk/post/sfc-obtains-worldwide-freezing-order-against-lo-kai-bong-and-major-success-group-limited) — `Enforcement`

- **23 Jun — Investor ID Regime consultation concluded (HKIDR-DM).** Broad support for extending investor ID to on-exchange derivatives; implementation targeted Q2 2028 alongside HKEX Orion platform. [Source](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=26PR97) — `Trade Monitoring & Surveillance` `Licensing & Supervision`

**Enforcement watch:** No further disciplinary/freezing/criminal items found beyond the above. Flagged for follow-up: ex-Yuanta Securities rep Wong Tim Hi suspended 9 months (3 Jul 2026–2 Apr 2027) re: unauthorised third-party trading (Ching Lee Holdings matter) — sits just outside this reporting window.

## HKMA

- **2 Jun — Circular: Cyber resilience amid AI-empowered threats.** Urges review of cyber risk management, incident response, recovery testing and third-party resilience — mirrors the SFC's same-week circular. [Source](https://brdr.hkma.gov.hk/eng/doc-ldg/docId/getPdf/20260529-8-EN/20260529-8-EN.pdf) — `Client Information Management` `Licensing & Supervision`

- **5 Jun — Joint HKMA/SFC consultation concluded: standardised OTC derivatives clearing calculation periods.** Fixed annual periods (1 Mar–31 May, 1 Sep–30 Nov) supported; legislative changes targeted from 1 Mar 2027. [Source](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/06/20260605-6/) — `Trade Monitoring — OTC Derivatives`
